# Project Title: Sales-Analysis 
PROJECT OBJECTIVES: Every business wants to increase sales revenue, but management often doesn't know: 
What customers typically buy.
How much customers spend.
Whether customers are buying enough items.
Why some customers spend significantly more than others.
How to encourage customers to increase their order size.

**What my Analysis Reveals** Customers Add More Items Than They Purchase,
Evidence:
Average Items in Cart = 5.49
Average Quantity Purchased = 2.95

This means customers place approximately 5 to 6 items in their carts but ultimately purchase only about 3 items.

**Business Concern**

Nearly half of the items considered by customers are not converted into actual sales.

Management may ask:

"Why are customers abandoning products before checkout?"

Possible Causes
Products are too expensive
Shipping costs are too high
Customers are comparing alternatives

**Business Impact**

Even a small increase in conversion could significantly increase revenue.

For example:

Current average purchase:

2.95 items

If increased to:

4 items

Revenue could rise without acquiring new customers.

## 📈 Key Insights & KPIs
* **Total Revenue:** £1,264,761.96
* **Top Product:** Chair is the top product with total value of £195,620.11

**Key Finding:** Customers Typically Purchase Small Quantities
The average quantity purchased is 2.95 items.
The median quantity is 3 items.
50% of all purchases contain between 2 and 4 items (Q1 = 2, Q3 = 4).

**Insight**
Most customers make relatively small purchases, indicating an opportunity to increase sales through cross-selling, upselling, or bundle offers.
